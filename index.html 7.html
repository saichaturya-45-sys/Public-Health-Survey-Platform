Responsible public health research

  • Collect only information needed for the stated purpose.
  • Explain participation, consent, and data use in plain language.
  • Avoid collecting directly identifying or sensitive health details unless necessary and properly protected.
  • Use access controls, encryption, backups, and a documented retention policy.
  • Obtain appropriate ethics and legal review before real-world deployment.
